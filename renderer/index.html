Bridge Equation

At balance, the Wheatstone bridge satisfies the cross-product condition:

\[\dfrac{R_1}{R_2} \;=\; \dfrac{R_s}{R_x}\]

Rearranging for the unknown resistor and the variable resistor, respectively:

\[R_x \;=\; \dfrac{R_2 \cdot R_s}{R_1}\]
\[R_s \;=\; \dfrac{R_x \cdot R_1}{R_2}\]
The supply voltage \(V_S\) does not appear in the balance equation. It affects only the voltmeter reading, the branch currents, and the power dissipated in the resistors.
